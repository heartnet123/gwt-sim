Model Assumptions, Governing Equations & Disclaimers

1. Goldak Double-Ellipsoid Volumetric Density: The power density distributed within the workpiece is formulated as:

qf,r(x,y,z) = [6√3 · ff,r · Q] / [a · b · cf,r · π√π] · exp(-3x²/cf,r² - 3y²/a² - 3z²/b²)

where a is semi-width, b is penetration depth, cf and cr are front and rear ellipsoid lengths, and ff + fr = 2 guarantees conservation of total effective thermal power Q = ηVI.

2. Conduction vs. Convection: The simulation computes 3D transient heat conduction with boundary radiation/convection heat losses. Fluid convection in the melt pool (Marangoni flow driven by surface tension gradients) is parameterized into effective thermal conductivity and the Goldak source shape.

3. Educational Scope: This interactive simulator is built for instructional purposes to help students and welding engineers visualize moving heat sources, transient thermal gradients, and the metallurgical significance of cooling times.
